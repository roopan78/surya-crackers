Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, mobile number, and your preferred pickup date and time — only what's needed to prepare your order for in-store pickup. If you choose to create an account, we also store your login mobile number so you can view your order history. We do not collect or store any payment details on this site — no payment is taken here. Our team arranges payment with you directly when they call to confirm your order.

How We Use It

Your order details are used solely to process and pack your order for pickup, and to contact you about it. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Local Storage & Cookies

This site stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage — your shopping cart contents, your 18+ age confirmation, and your login session if you sign in — so they persist between visits. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us until you actually place an order or log in.

Data Retention & Your Rights

Order records are retained for accounting and warranty purposes. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us directly.
