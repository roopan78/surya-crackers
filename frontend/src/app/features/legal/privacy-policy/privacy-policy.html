Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, delivery address, phone number and preferred delivery date — only what's needed to fulfil and deliver your order. We do not require an account or collect payment card details through this site; orders are confirmed and paid for directly with us over WhatsApp.

How We Use It

Your order details are used solely to process, pack and deliver your order, and to contact you about it. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Local Storage & Cookies

This site stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage — your shopping cart contents and your 18+ age confirmation — so they persist between visits. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us until you actually place an order.

Data Retention & Your Rights

Order records are retained for accounting and warranty purposes. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us directly.
