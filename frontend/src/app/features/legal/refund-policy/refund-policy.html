Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

When a Refund Applies

Refunds are issued only in the following cases: your order is cancelled before it has been dispatched, an item arrives damaged in transit, or you receive an incorrect item. Because firecrackers are combustible, regulated goods, we are unable to accept refunds for change-of-mind or after an order has left our facility in good condition.

How to Request One

Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the damaged or incorrect item. We will confirm the issue and let you know whether a refund, replacement, or store credit applies.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method used at the time of order, or adjusted against a future order if you prefer.
