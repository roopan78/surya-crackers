Return Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Why Returns Are Limited

Firecrackers and fireworks are age-restricted, combustible products regulated under explosives licensing law. For safety and legal reasons, we cannot accept returns of products once they have left our facility in undamaged, correct condition — this applies even to unopened boxes.

Exceptions

We will arrange a replacement, free of charge, if the item handed to you at pickup doesn't match what you ordered, the packaging was damaged or tampered with, or the product is defective. Report this to us over WhatsApp within 48 hours of pickup with photos.

Order Cancellations

You may cancel or modify an order any time before it has been packed for pickup by messaging us directly on WhatsApp with your order number. Once an order has been packed and marked ready for pickup, it can no longer be cancelled.
