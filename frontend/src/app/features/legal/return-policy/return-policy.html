Return Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Why Returns Are Limited

Firecrackers and fireworks are age-restricted, combustible products regulated under explosives licensing law. For safety and legal reasons, we cannot accept returns of products once they have left our facility in undamaged, correct condition — this applies even to unopened boxes.

Exceptions

We will arrange a return and replacement, free of charge, if: the item delivered doesn't match what you ordered, the packaging was damaged or tampered with in transit, or the product is defective on arrival. Report this to us over WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos.

Order Cancellations

You may cancel or modify an order any time before it is dispatched by messaging us directly on WhatsApp with your order number. Once an order has been packed and handed to our delivery partner, it can no longer be cancelled.
